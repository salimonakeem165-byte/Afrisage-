Our purpose

A home for African knowledge and imagination.

AfriSage — Where Stories Live, Knowledge Travels.

What we are building

AfriSage is an African digital reading and publishing platform. Our goal is to connect authors and readers through books that can be discovered, purchased, downloaded and read online.

Our principles

  • Respect authors and their creative work.
  • Make reading and knowledge more accessible.
  • Use transparent editorial review and publication approval.
  • Build a welcoming home for African voices.
