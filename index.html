Our publishing promise

Every manuscript is reviewed before publication. Final publishing decisions remain with the AfriSage owner. The planned author revenue share is 80% to the author and 20% to AfriSage, subject to published terms and payment-provider fees.

Guardian AI will assist screening; automated assessments are not infallible and should not replace human review.

About AfriSage

A home for African knowledge and imagination.

AfriSage is an African digital reading and publishing centre connecting authors with readers worldwide. Our aim is to celebrate African literature, knowledge and creativity through an accessible digital library.
